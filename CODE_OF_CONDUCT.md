# Code of Conduct

SoloC is made by **SoloGem** for beginners and builders. We want every learner and contributor to feel welcome.

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in SoloC a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity, experience level, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity.

## Our standards

**Do this:**

- Be kind, patient, and clear — especially with newcomers
- Give and accept constructive feedback
- Focus on what helps people learn SoloC
- Assume good intent

**Don't do this:**

- Insults, harassment, or personal attacks
- Trolling or deliberate disruption
- Sharing others' private information
- Anything that makes learning feel unsafe

## Enforcement

Report concerns to **community@sologem.dev**. SoloGem will review reports and take action that feels fair — from a private reminder to removing someone from community spaces.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in SoloC spaces: GitHub, chats, docs comments, and events — online or offline.

Thanks for helping SoloC stay the friendliest place to learn to code.
